\section{Contributions}

Our contributions in this work are twofold: First, we demonstrate a novel inversion method against AdaRound to recover coarse outlines that represent its calibration data, motivating the need to consider privacy in data-dependent quantization method. Second, we propose a gradient-aware adaptive rounding method based on AdaRound, and empirically validate the proposed method on two language models.
